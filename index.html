    How I work

    From a blank brief to a video that is live in the ad account, usually within a few days. Then the numbers decide the next version.

    01

    Concept & script

    I find the angle and write the script myself: the first 3 seconds, the trend to ride, and several variants worth testing.

    02

    AI video production

    Veo 3, Kling and Seedance for text-to-video and image-to-video, with prompts that keep characters, scenes and camera consistent.

    03

    Edit, motion & sound

    AI output becomes a finished ad in Premiere Pro and After Effects: cut, motion, captions and sound design. Templates in Remotion when volume matters.

    04

    Performance loop

    I read CTR, CVR, ROAS and revenue per video with the UA team, then iterate on what scales and kill what does not.
